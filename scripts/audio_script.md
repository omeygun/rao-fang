# Thai audio recording script [MANUAL]

Generated by `scripts/gen_audio_script.py` from the app's template config — re-run it if templates change.

**Who:** a native Central Thai speaker (Noor or her daughter is ideal — a familiar voice). Do **not** use synthetic TTS for these files.

**How:** record each line as a separate file, named exactly as in the *file* column, in mono, mp3 (64–96 kbps) or m4a/AAC. Quiet room, phone ~20 cm from the mouth. Leave ~0.1 s of silence at each end and speak at a calm, even pace — clips are played back-to-back to make sentences such as “แขก · 5 · คนชอบ · การชิมกาแฟ”. Keep the intonation neutral (not sentence-final) for fragments that sit mid-sentence.

Put the files in `app/public/audio/th/`, then run `python scripts/gen_audio_script.py --scan` to build the manifest. Target total < 5 MB (spec §16.3).
